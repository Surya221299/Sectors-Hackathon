# Privacy Policy for Invelio

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

Welcome to **Invelio** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how information is handled when you use our mobile application ("Invelio" or the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Invelio. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Information You Provide Directly
* **Search Queries & Chat Inputs:** When you interact with our AI financial assistant or search for tickers, your text queries are processed in real-time to generate relevant market insights and responses.
* **Portfolio & Watchlist Preferences:** Any virtual portfolios, simulated trades, or stock watchlists you create are stored to provide app functionality and personalization.

### b. Information Collected Automatically
* **Diagnostic & Usage Data:** We may collect anonymous crash reports and device performance metrics (such as OS version, device model, and app version) to fix bugs and improve stability.
* **We do NOT collect:**
  * Real financial account numbers, brokerage credentials, or banking details.
  * Precise geolocation data.
  * Sensitive personal identification (e.g., government IDs, phone numbers, or physical addresses).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for:
* Providing core app functionalities (displaying real-time market data, AI stock scores, charts, and anomaly alerts).
* Processing AI conversational queries with grounded financial information.
* Improving app performance, user experience, and fixing crashes.
* We do **NOT** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties.
* We do **NOT** serve third-party targeted advertisements.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and AI Integration

Invelio utilizes trusted third-party services to deliver financial data and cognitive intelligence:
* **Sectors Financial API & MCP:** Used to retrieve verified Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) market data, financial ratios, news sentiment, and historical prices.
* **Google Gemini AI & LLM Services:** Used for processing natural language financial queries and generating qualitative equity theses. Query prompts sent to the AI do not contain personal user identity.
* **Apple Services:** For app distribution, crash reporting, and platform diagnostics.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our backend services. However, please remember that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Invelio is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
